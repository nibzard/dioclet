# V0.10 level 2: map size and faction cards (2026-11-14)

Research date: 2026-11-14. Sources: `tools/v10_level2_experiment.py`,
`results/v10-level2/confirm.json` (seeds 1030000+),
`results/v10-level2/holdout.json` (seeds 1040000+). Every arm has
1,536 games. All players are bots (collector, early_support,
prestige_farmer, counterleader on the 0.9.4 seat schedule).

## The questions

The designer asked these questions:

- Is the 13 × 9 succession map too small, so that level 2 ends too
  quickly?
- Can level 2 use the palace board's size, so that one real board
  flips over?
- Are the fixed faction sites fair to every gate?
- Do faction cards help? The proposal uses three faction cards and
  three blanks, dealt face down like the level 1 treasures, so that
  the first search misses half the time.

## Arms

Every other rule is 0.9.4.

| Arm | Map | Gates | Factions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v094` | 13 × 9 | 4/6/4/6 steps from the Peristyle | public, fixed |
| `big_public` | 19 × 13, four blocks | 6/9/6/9 | public, fixed |
| `big_blind` | 19 × 13 | 6/9/6/9 | cards on six sites |
| `flip_blind` | the palace grid itself | palace gates | cards on the six district entries |
| `small_pin_public` | 13 × 9 | pinwheel, all 6 | public, fixed |
| `big_pin_public` | 19 × 13 | pinwheel, all 9 | public, fixed |
| `small_pin_blind` | 13 × 9 | pinwheel, all 6 | cards on six sites |
| `big_pin_blind` | 19 × 13 | pinwheel, all 9 | cards on six sites |

"Pinwheel" means that each gate sits off-centre on its side, turned
the same way. Then every gate is the same street distance from the
Peristyle. The card sites were chosen by a geometric search (scratch
script, not kept). Two conditions applied:

- The sites look the same after a half-turn of the board.
- Every gate sees the same sorted distances to the sites.

On the big map each gate sees the six sites at 4, 6, 14, 14, 14 and
24 steps. The sites are 5 or 15 steps from the Peristyle.

## Verified findings

Confirmation run (seeds 1030000+):

| Arm | Draws | L2 p10/p25/med/p75/p90 | Total median | Gates N/E/S/W | Uniformity p | Farmer wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v094 | .004 | 5/6/8/11/15 | 23 | .272/.227/.266/.232 | .023 | .120 |
| big_public | .003 | 6/6/8/11/15 | 23 | .280/.239/.240/.239 | .053 | .122 |
| big_blind | .019 | 7/8/11/14/18 | 26 | .262/.199/.294/.227 | <.001 | .191 |
| flip_blind | .026 | 8/10/12/15/20 | 27 | .283/.172/.215/.303 | <.001 | .165 |
| small_pin_public | .008 | 5/7/9/12/16 | 24 | .328/.174/.184/.307 | <.001 | .107 |
| big_pin_public | .008 | 6/7/9/12/16 | 24 | .348/.156/.157/.331 | <.001 | .113 |
| small_pin_blind | .014 | 6/7/9/12/17 | 24 | .253/.234/.248/.251 | .69 | .220 |
| big_pin_blind | .019 | 8/10/12/15/20 | 27 | .251/.242/.244/.243 | .97 | .141 |

Holdout run on the finalists (seeds 1040000+):

| Arm | Draws | L2 p10/p25/med/p75/p90 | Total median | Gates N/E/S/W | Uniformity p | Farmer wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v094 | .009 | 5/6/8/11/15 | 23 | .275/.218/.262/.236 | .007 | .122 |
| small_pin_blind | .010 | 6/7/9/13/17 | 25 | .247/.233/.266/.244 | .33 | .211 |
| big_pin_blind | .029 | 8/10/12/15/19 | 27 | .255/.236/.236/.245 | .67 | .160 |

1. **Level 2 is not too quick on any map.** No game in any arm ended
   level 2 in 4 rounds or fewer. The rules set a floor of about 5
   rounds: two supports, then a claim that survives two rounds.
2. **Map size alone does not lengthen level 2.** The big map with
   public factions has the same median as 0.9.4 (8 rounds). Players
   walk farther, but each site is still a known target.
3. **Faction cards add about 3 to 4 rounds.** Bots search about 5.5
   to 5.8 times a game.
4. **0.9.4 is not fair to every gate.** The east and west gates are
   6 steps from the Peristyle, and the north and south gates are 4.
   Both runs reject uniformity (p = .023 and p = .007). This revises
   the "uniform at 384 games" result in D50, which had too little
   power to see a gap of about 5 points.
5. **Fairness depends on the whole route.** The route goes from the
   gate to the sites, then to the Peristyle. Equal gate distances
   alone fail (`*_pin_public`). Three public sites cannot be
   symmetric under a half-turn, so two gates always gain.
6. **Flipping the palace grid as it is fails.** `flip_blind` has the
   worst east rate (.172), because the palace gates are not the same
   distance from the Peristyle.
7. **Pinwheel gates with six card sites pass in both runs.** The big
   map has p = .97 and .67, with a gate spread of 1 to 2 points.
8. **Cards help the Prestige farmer.** Its win share rises from .12
   (0.9.4) to .14 to .16 on the big map and .21 to .22 on the small
   map. A fair share is .25.

## Decision

The designer chose the big 19 × 13 map (2026-11-14), because it can go
on the back of the real board. It is promoted as
`rules_v010_bigboard.json` ("0.10.0-bigboard"). See D51.

## Open questions

- **Draws.** The big map draws in 1.9% (confirmation) and 2.9%
  (holdout) of games, against 0.4 to 0.9% for 0.9.4. Watch this in
  live play. If it grows, a shorter claim window or a cheaper search
  are the first knobs.
- **Model and human play.** In an earlier test, model players did not
  find hidden sites on the palace map. A four-seat Haiku game on the
  big map is the first check
  (`runs/haiku-table-v010/`).
- **The physical board.** The print kit and the art still draw the
  0.9.4 succession map. Both need the new map and six card spaces.
- **The browser engine** still does not implement `board_level2`.
