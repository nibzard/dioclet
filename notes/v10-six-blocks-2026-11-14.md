# V0.10 six-block succession map (2026-11-14)

Research date: 2026-11-14. Question from the designer: the succession
map has four blocks but six faction cards. Can it use six blocks (the
palace's 3 x 2 grid), one card per block, and stay fair?

Sources:

- `tools/v10_six_blocks.py`: the geometry search (`search`), the arm
  files (`write`), and the bot runs (`screen`).
- `results/v10-six-blocks/screen-1070000-768.json` (768 games per arm).
- `results/v10-six-blocks/holdout-1080000-1536.json` (1,536 games per
  arm, fresh seeds).
- Arm files: `rules_v10_six_spread.json`, `rules_v10_six_ring.json`,
  `rules_v10_six_near.json`. Each is 0.10.1 with only the succession
  map changed.

## Geometry

The six-block map uses the palace streets: rows 0, 6 and 12, and
columns 0, 6, 12 and 18 (97 street spaces). The 0.10.1 gates stay
where they are. The Peristyle is (9,6), the centre of the middle
street, so every gate is 9 steps from it.

- No fair set exists if each card must touch only its own block.
- 20 sets are fair if each block gets exactly one card. A card may sit
  on a street between two blocks.
- In all 20 sets, the two middle blocks get their cards on the
  Peristyle's street, 1 or 2 steps from it. The geometry forces this.

## Verified findings

1. **Six blocks are fair.** All three arms pass the gate test in both
   runs (p = .15 to .64).
2. **Level 2 gets about 2 rounds shorter.** The median falls from 12
   to 10 rounds; p90 falls from 18 to 16. Two cards always sit next to
   the Peristyle, so support is closer.
3. **The Prestige farmer loses what the posts gave it.** Its win
   share falls from .22 to .16 or .17, which is about four standard
   errors. This is the level that 0.10.0 had before the posts.
4. **The lockout measures do not change.** No-contest claims stay at
   about 2%, and turns at 0 Prestige stay at 35 to 39%.
5. Draws stay low (0.7 to 1.2%). Bots search about 5.9 times a game
   on every map.

## Open questions

- Is a shorter level 2 good? The bot median on 0.10.1 is 12 rounds,
  and the Haiku game on 0.10.0 took 9. Shorter play may be welcome,
  but it gives the farmer less time.
- Two cards next to the Peristyle: does that feel right to people?
  The first player to search there gets support very close to the
  Peristyle.
- Looks: both sides of the board would have the same block layout.
  That is simpler to make, but players could confuse the two sides.
- If adopted, the BW map, the art map (four quarters) and the prose
  need redrawing for six blocks.
