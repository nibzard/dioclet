Research date: 2026-11-14. Sources:

- `tools/v10_prestige_screen.py`: one-knob screen, 768 games per arm,
  seeds 1050000+. Results in `results/v10-prestige/screen-1050000-5.json`
  and `screen-1050000-3.json`.
- `tools/v10_prestige_experiment.py`: holdout, 1,536 games per arm,
  seeds 1060000+. Results in `results/v10-prestige/holdout.json`.
- `runs/haiku-table-v010/game1`: the four-seat Haiku game on the big

All arms use `rules_v010_bigboard.json`. Each arm changes one knob.

## The problem

In the Haiku game, P1 proclaimed at turn 85. At that moment all three
rivals held 0 Prestige. They duelled each other to earn the contest fee.
P0 earned 1 Prestige and spent it on support. P3 and P2 earned 1 each,
but only in the last round of the window. Nobody contested, and P1 won
at turn 92. Human session 001 reported the same lockout.

the session found 16 fair sets of four posts; this is the outer-ring set.
That search script is not in the repo. `tests/test_v010_bigboard.py`
checks only the chosen set.

## Holdout (1,536 games, seeds 1060000+)

"Probes" are the four frozen profiles. A "spender" is the same
profile, but while a rival's claim stands it buys support whenever it
can. This copies the Haiku seats' mistake.

## Verified findings

1. **Cheaper or longer contests are the wrong direction.** Each one
   lets rivals cancel claims over and over. Draws rise to 16–39% and
   level 2 runs to the round cap.
2. **Guard posts help, but not by fixing the lockout.** With the posts,
   the share of claims that no rival can contest stays at about 2–3%,
   and turns at 0 Prestige fall only from 37–39% to 34–35%. The gain is
   elsewhere: fewer draws, a fair share for the Prestige farmer (.22–.25
   against .16–.20), and gate fairness.
3. **Spending during a claim breaks gate fairness without posts.** With
   spender seats, the base board skews to the north gate (.290 against
   .212 west, p = .0001). With posts it is fair (p = .56). Spenders also
   make claims stick: 2.2 claims per game against 4.0.
4. **The bots do not reproduce the lockout.** Even spender seats leave
   1.6–1.8 rivals able to contest each claim. The Haiku seats had 0.
   The rest of the gap is in how people and agents play, so the
   "keep 1 Prestige in reserve" line in the quick reference and the
   rulebook is the direct answer to it.
5. The base board's own gate test gave p = .046 in this holdout, against
   p = .97 and .67 in the D51 runs. Across three tests of the same
   layout, one value near .05 is expected by chance; watch it.

## Recommendation

Adopt the four guard posts on the succession map, and print the reserve
line. Keep the fee, the duel-loser cost, and the two-round window.

## Open questions

- Does the reserve line change how people play? Only a human session
  or a model-player game with the new quick reference can show it.
- If players still reach a claim with 0 Prestige, a stronger rule is to
  give each rival at 0 Prestige 1 Prestige when a claim is proclaimed.
  The engine does not support it, and it is close to a free contest, so
  test it for draws first.
- Adopting the posts changes the print kit: the succession map needs
  four guard posts, and the prose "no guard posts on this map" must go.
