# V0.9.4 twin-board design

Research date: 2026-11-13.

Sources: the user's review request (faction sites too close to gates;
two printed boards), the 2026-11-12 playtest's Level 2 redesign
(`playtest/human-session-001.md`), the geometry sweep
(`notes/v09-geometry-sweep-2026-11-13.md`), and the twin-board
screens in `results/v09-twinboard/`.

## The complaint, measured

On the single board the faction sites sat where the palace map put
them: elites 3 steps from the north gate (spread 18 across gates),
officials beside the throne, army near the south-east rim. The
playtest had already redesigned this at the table: a smaller second
board, everyone back to gates, meeting places placed deliberately.

## The design

- `board_level2`: 13x9, four blocks, streets at x=0,6,12 and
  y=0,4,8. Gates P0 (6,0), P1 (12,4), P2 (6,8), P3 (0,4). The
  Peristyle at the crossing (6,4). Cardo gates stand 4 steps from
  the crossing, decumanus gates 6 - a two-pair split, the tightest
  the street grid allows.
- Phase transition: every seat returns to its gate ON THE NEW MAP
  (the playtest's table rule, now structural).
- Meeting places (twinB, the confirmed set): elites (6,3) on the
  north cardo, officials (3,4) on the west decumanus, army (9,4) on
  the east decumanus - one per street arm, none on the Peristyle.
  Every gate's nearest site is 3 to 5 steps out (the playtest's
  fairness bar), and no site sits at a gate.
- No glory posts on the succession map: duel wins are the phase-2
  income, and the engine keeps `board_level2.glory_posts` as a
  validated knob if a floor is ever needed.
- Support exists only on the succession map; phase 1 is pure
  collection. The engine exposes the active board to players and
  policies, so no policy code needed changes beyond two empty-post
  guards.

## Verified findings (96 matched seeds 1000000+, 384 fresh 1010000+)

| Arm | Draws | L1/L2 medians | Claims/game | Gate win rates | Farmer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9.3 single board | 1/384 | 17/6 | 4.88 | .276/.193/.287/.242 | .167 |
| twinA (ring) | 2/384 | 15/8 | 4.64 | .245/.219/.273/.258 | .099 |
| twinB (triangle) | 2/384 | 15/8 | 4.45 | .266/.250/.242/.237 | .122 |

1. **The east-gate skew is solved structurally.** twinB's gate
   spread is 2.9 points with the east gate exactly at the uniform
   rate; the single board's east gate stays at .193. The reset to
   equidistant-by-pair gates does what placement tweaks could not.
2. **The endgame earns its rounds.** Phase 2 takes 8 rounds instead
   of 6: the support hunt is real movement, not a sidebar.
3. **Collection intensifies.** With support gone from phase 1,
   district exploration rises and the set completes two rounds
   earlier. The first claimant still wins about a third of games.
4. **The farmer profile weakens (.17 to .12).** No posts on the
   succession map means Prestige comes from duels only in phase 2.
   The 0.08 floor holds; watch it in the human session.
5. twinA (sites clustered at the crossing) passes too but with a
   thinner farmer and wider spread; twinB's one-site-per-arm layout
   is both the better measurement and the better table fiction.
