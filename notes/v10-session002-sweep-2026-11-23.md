## Verified findings

1. **Session 002's table misplays multiplied the Prestige lockout by 7x.**
   Turning duels into zero-sum loser-to-winner transfers and reading `4+`
   as `5+` (`table_misplay`) pushed uncontestable claims from 7.1% to
   48.6% (47.1% under spender stress), raised L2 turns at 0 Prestige from
   44.5% to 69.8%, and collapsed contests from 7.78 to 0.90 per game.
   Clarifying `"from the bank"` on the player mat removes the bulk of
   that failure mode before touching any mechanic.
2. **Post-D56 baseline draws sit at 13.0% under a 2-full-round window.**
   D56 extended the engine's claim window by one full round to match the
   print; under bots, draws rose from ~1% pre-D56 to 13.0% (3.1% with
   spender seats).
3. **`pilum2_window1` is the cleanest mechanical package in the sweep.**
   Under `bonus_per_side: "best"`, the baseline Javelin (`pilum` +1) gives
   a strict bonus over the Sword (`gladius` +1) in only 2.0% of contests.
   Raising Javelin to `+2` vs the claimant under window=2 fixes the item
   (97.9% active advantage) but drives draws to 22.7%. Pairing Javelin
   `+2` with `claim_window_rounds: 1` (survive 1 full round after the
   proclaim round — the pre-D56 timing) keeps draws at 0.8%, tightens L2
   to 7/10/17 rounds, hits `gate_p = 0.80` (.260/.260/.240/.232), preserves
   3.83 claims/game, and makes Javelin matter in 98.5% of contests.
4. **Public Level-2 Helmet (`galea_l2_public`) is safe and far more used.**
   Baseline bots use Helmet only 0.05 times/game in the Palace. Allowing
   an unused Helmet to publicly reveal one face-down faction card in Level
   2 raises usage to 1.77/game, trims draws (13.0% → 10.9%), leaves L2
   duration and gate fairness untouched (`p = 0.26`), and preserves the
   excitement humans enjoyed in Session 002 without private-info
   asymmetry.
5. **Free contests and proclaim-time coin floors both cause denial loops.**
   `proclaim_floor_1` (the open question from `v10-prestige-2026-11-14.md`)
   spikes draws to 34.9% (65.9% with Javelin +2); `fee0_window1` still
   hits 32.3% draws even at window=1. Gentle income relief
   (`guard_roll_3plus` or `transition_stipend_1`) is safe: draws edge
   down to 10.9–12.0%, uncontestable claims drop to 5.0–5.9%, and gates
   stay fair.
6. **Armor (`lorica_segmentata`) directly drives Peristyle re-claim loops.**
   In baseline play, Armor saves a beaten claimant 1.74 times/game, and
   67.2% of those saves are followed by the same player immediately
   proclaiming again on their next turn (88.7% under `table_misplay`),
   reproducing the exact pile-up observed in Session 002.

## Candidates for a V0.11 rules pass (for owner decision)

- **Print-only (already applied):** English primary card names with Latin
  inscribed on the artwork plinth; player-mat fixes for bank-paid duels,
  ordinary-duel stay-put, Palace-only public Helmet, `+1 round` grace
  reminder, and pawn pass-through.
- **Strongest mechanical candidates to consider next:**
  1. `galea_l2_public` (Helmet works once in either phase, public reveal)
  2. `pilum2_window1` (Javelin `+2` vs claimant paired with 1 full round
     post-proclaim window)
  3. Optional gentle Prestige floor: `transition_stipend_1` or
     `guard_roll_3plus` (avoid `proclaim_floor_1` and `contest_fee: 0`).
