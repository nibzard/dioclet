# Playtest comparison: V0.8 fee 1 versus the bonus-1 candidate

Protocol only. No playtest session has taken place. Do not record
invented participants, scores, or quotes. When sessions happen, record
them in `playtest/protocol-v08.md` per that file's rules.

## Arms

- **A (live rules):** `rules_v08_succession.json` — contest fee 1,
  optional boost +2 for 1 Prestige. This is the deployed rulebook and
  the printed pack. No preparation needed.
- **B (candidate):** identical except the optional boost is +1 for
  1 Prestige (`BEST_CANDIDATE.json`, hash `8bc693e9…`). Prepare a
  matching printed quick reference that changes only that number.

Simulation says B cuts canceled claims by about a fifth with
counterplay unchanged. It cannot say which version is more fun or
easier to learn. That is what this comparison decides.

## Design

- Counterbalanced, blind to the hypothesis. Players are told the two
  versions differ in one number, not which or why.
- Groups, in order of value: a family with children, adult
  non-gamers, adults who enjoy strategy games, older first-time
  players. Two groups minimum for any conclusion.
- Each group plays game 1 with one arm and game 2 with the other.
  Alternate the starting arm across groups. Rotate gates and first
  player between games using the protocol's rotation sheet.
- Same teacher, same script, same printed pack layout for both arms.

## What to record per game (from protocol-v08.md)

Actual teach time in minutes. Real finish time. Clarification count.
Whether players can state the win condition uncoached. Whether
players can state what the boost does and what it costs. Every boost
purchase: who, when, and (after the game) why. Every canceled claim:
one sentence on how the claimant reacted. Turns players name as
having two appealing options. Waiting or repetitive turns. Desire to
replay, and intent to change strategy next time.

## Arm-specific questions

- Arm A: when a player holds 2 Prestige at a claim response, is
  buying the +2 boost automatic? Do they still feel they chose?
- Arm B: at +1, do players still buy the boost? Do they explain a
  reason either way ("the leader has more gear", "I need it for
  support")?
- Both: after a canceled claim, does the claimant re-engage or
  disengage?

## Decision rule (agree before the first session)

Keep the live rules if the candidate is harder to teach, provokes
repeated frustration, or produces no difference players can name.
Adopt the candidate only if players understand it as easily and at
least one group reports the claim fights as better. A statistical tie
goes to the simpler-reading version, which is arm A unless teaching
notes say otherwise.
