Diocletian's Palace

Print-and-play playtest pack. Unofficial prototype for tabletop testing. Print these pages at 100% scale. You need, besides this pack: two ordinary six-sided dice for movement and one six-sided die for fights and trials. Cut out the cards and tokens on the later pages. Any small object (coin, meeple, button) works better than paper pawns if you have one per player.

What is in the pack

  1. The board (all 107 street spaces; its page prints landscape, and A3 paper makes the pieces comfortable).
  2. Six treasure location cards.
  3. Tokens: Glory, seals, Emperor hits, pawns.
  4. Four equipment tracker sheets.
  5. The rules (two pages).
  6. One-page quick reference.
  7. Five-minute teaching script.
  8. Feedback form.

Rules version: V0.6.2 real board, 2d6 movement. Level 2 site placements on this board are the design team's current tested choice, not final artwork.

The rules

Goal

Be the first to land two hits on the Emperor.

Setup

Each player picks a gate (all four are equal). Put your pawn on it. Shuffle the six treasure cards face down, one on each blue entry space. Choose a starting player; the start moves one seat clockwise every round.

Your turn

  1. Roll both movement dice. The sum is your maximum steps, not an exact count.
  2. Move along the street circles, orthogonally, up to your roll. You may stop early. You may pass through other pawns.
  3. Do one thing where you stand (or just end your move): take a treasure card, stand a guard post, duel a rival, or later claim a seal or strike the Emperor.
  4. Guard posts and duels always need a move of at least one space first. You cannot use either of them twice from the same spot without moving away and coming back.

The city (first half)

  • Blue entry spaces: end your move there and take that block's treasure card. Every card can be taken by every player once. Treasure is never stolen or lost.
  • Guard posts (green *): end your move there and roll one die: 4, 5 or 6 earns 1 Glory.
  • Duels: end your move within two street spaces of a rival to challenge them. Each of you rolls one die and adds a gear bonus (below). Higher total wins, ties to the attacker. The winner takes 1 Glory. Nothing else changes hands.
  • Gear bonus: 0-2 cards +0, 3-5 cards +1, all 6 cards +2.
  • Glory: hold at most 2. Beyond that it is lost.

The gate

The first player who owns all six treasures and reaches the gold checkpoint takes 1 Glory and opens the gate. Finish that round, then one more full round in the city. Then everyone missing cards gets them for free, all pawns return to their gates, and the palace begins. If nobody opens the gate by the end of round 24, it opens anyway.

The palace (second half)

You need two different seals before the Emperor will face you. Duels still work exactly as in the city.

SealHow to earn it
Cellars (c)Roll one die: 3 or more.
Barracks (b)You roll die + gear bonus, the guard rolls die +1. Beat the guard.
Peristyle (p)Roll one die: 5 or 6. Or pay 1 Glory, no roll.

The Emperor

  1. Stand in his chamber (red E) and strike: you roll die + gear bonus, he rolls die +2. Score higher to hit.
  2. You may declare a powered strike before rolling: pay 1 Glory for +2, paid even on a miss.
  3. Hit: mark it, stay, strike again next turn. Miss: step back to the retreat hall. Nothing else is lost.
  4. First to two hits wins.

Everything is public: positions, cards, Glory, seals, hits. Only the face-down treasures and the next roll are hidden.

Five-minute teaching script

  1. Point at the board. "This is a Roman palace. You enter through your gate. The streets are the circles; the red blocks are buildings, you never walk there."
  2. The race. "Two halves. First, grab gear in the city. Then the gate opens and everyone races to hit the Emperor twice."
  3. Show a turn. "Roll both dice, move up to the total, then do one thing. That's a whole turn."
  4. Show the three city things. Stop on a blue space, take that treasure. Stop on a green star, roll 4+ for Glory. End next to a rival, duel: one die each plus your gear bonus, winner takes a Glory.
  5. Glory. "You can hold two. It buys exactly two things: the safe seal, or +2 on an Emperor strike."
  6. The gate. "First player with all six treasures opens it; everyone else gets their missing cards free, so nobody is left behind."
  7. The palace. "Two different seals, then into the chamber: beat his die by yours plus bonus. A miss only steps you back one space. Two hits and the game is yours."
  8. Start. "Highest roll of one die moves first, and the start rotates every round."

Read the quick reference aloud once, then start. Answer the rest as it comes up.
